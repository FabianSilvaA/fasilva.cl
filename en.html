Profile

Research, Teaching and Applied Work

I am an academic and researcher specializing in the development and application of decision-support models for organizations. My work integrates Operations Research, optimization, Artificial Intelligence, and Machine Learning to address complex challenges in management, planning, prioritization, and resource allocation.
